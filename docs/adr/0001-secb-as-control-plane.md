# ADR-0001: SecB Is the Governed Control Plane

- **Status:** Proposed / Not Effective
- **Date:** 2026-07-17
- **Decision:** SecB owns identity, policy, authority, workflow, evidence acceptance, knowledge/skill promotion, and human governance. External tools remain systems of record for their native domains.
- **Rationale:** A monolithic super-agent cannot provide reliable separation of duties, provider independence, or independent evidence acceptance.
- **Consequences:** More integration contracts and governance overhead; substantially stronger accountability, portability, and auditability.
