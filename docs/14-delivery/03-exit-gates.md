# Exit Gates

## Foundation gates

- Canonical IDs and schemas validate.
- Project, module, goal and work package are traceable.
- Agent, role, harness and model are server-derived.
- Risk and mutation classes resolve deterministically.
- Context Receipt and evidence destination exist.

## Execution gates

- Workspace and baseline are isolated and pinned.
- Declared and observed write sets reconcile.
- Tool, network and credential use conforms.
- Checkpoints and retries are durable and attributable.
- Unauthorized actions produce containment and failure evidence.

## Assurance gates

- Producer verification is fresh.
- REV and QA satisfy independence level.
- Security findings are resolved or accepted by authority.
- Evidence integrity and provenance validate.
- Candidate is immutable before integration review.

## Delivery gates

- Composite tests pass against latest integration head.
- Documentation, migrations and rollback are complete.
- Human approval is present where required.
- Outcome observation window and owner are defined.

## Learning gates

- Non-success events have structured disposition.
- Outcome Receipt exists.
- Experience candidates are accepted, rejected or deferred.
- Knowledge claims include scope, evidence and validity.
- Skill candidates enter evaluation rather than direct publication.
