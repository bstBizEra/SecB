# Definition of Done

**Document ID:** SECB-DOD-001
**Version:** 1.0.0-draft
**Status:** DRAFT / NOT EFFECTIVE

A Work Package is done only when:

- objective, scope, and acceptance criteria are satisfied;
- final artifacts match the authorized baseline and write set;
- required documentation and ADRs are updated;
- deterministic checks pass after the final change;
- negative and adversarial cases required by risk class pass;
- no unresolved blocking REV, QA, SEC, DOMAIN, or OPS finding remains;
- evidence is sealed, independently verified, and accepted;
- role independence is proven;
- residual risks and limitations are disclosed;
- rollback/recovery is tested where applicable;
- no unauthorized path, dependency, network, model, tool, MCP, skill, data, or credential use occurred;
- outcome measurement is defined or completed as required;
- the authorized governance transition is recorded; and
- the final status report links every material claim to evidence.

`Code written`, `document drafted`, `tests previously passed`, `agent reported success`, and `review requested` are progress states, not completion.
