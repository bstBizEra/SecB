# KPIs and Scorecards

## Platform

- governed session completion rate;
- evidence completeness and acceptance;
- mean recovery time;
- policy violation and containment rate;
- cost per accepted work package;
- outcome realization rate.

## Delivery

- critical-path reduction;
- parallel efficiency;
- rework and late-conflict rate;
- integration queue time;
- defect escape;
- rollback frequency and recovery success.

## Agents and harnesses

- acceptance rate;
- independent review finding rate;
- tool success and unsupported-claim rate;
- cost, latency and context efficiency;
- evidence quality;
- safety/control violations.

## Learning and skills

- failures with structured disposition;
- experience-to-knowledge conversion;
- knowledge contradiction/supersession closure;
- skill candidate evaluation pass rate;
- skill reuse, regression and outcome improvement;
- time from repeated failure to governed skill.
