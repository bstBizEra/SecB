# Governing Principles

1. **Outcome over activity** — optimize accepted outcomes, not agent utilization or token volume.
2. **Control before autonomy** — autonomy is granted after identity, scope, evidence and recovery are defined.
3. **Evidence before authority transition** — state changes depend on verifiable propositions.
4. **Minimum sufficient context** — context is scoped, current and receipted.
5. **Minimum sufficient team** — roles scale with risk and complexity.
6. **Independent challenge** — important claims face review outside the producer session.
7. **Failure is data** — every meaningful non-success produces a structured learning disposition.
8. **Knowledge is temporal** — claims have scope, validity, contradiction and supersession.
9. **Skills are governed software** — skills have versions, tests, permissions and retirement.
10. **Human accountability remains explicit** — systems advise and execute within bounded authority; humans own governance-root decisions.
