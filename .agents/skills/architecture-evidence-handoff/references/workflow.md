# Detailed Workflow — Architecture Evidence and Handoff

## Preconditions

- Confirm: completed architecture activity.
- Confirm: artifacts and evidence.
- Confirm: decisions and assumptions.
- Confirm: destination role and purpose.

## Detailed checks

- Identify source role, destination role, objective, scope, baseline, and authority boundary.
- List completed work, artifacts, decisions, evidence, and verified facts.
- List assumptions, inferences, limitations, unresolved findings, risks, and changed context.
- State required next actions, acceptance criteria, verification obligations, and escalation triggers.
- Provide direct references and integrity digests where available.
- Minimize context while preserving material decision and evidence links.
- Require the destination role to verify evidence rather than trust the handoff narrative.

## Anti-patterns

- Starting implementation before the architecture scope and authority are established.
- Treating plausible inference as verified fact.
- Hiding uncertainty or adverse consequences.
- Using a framework mechanically when it does not answer the stakeholder question.
- Declaring approval, conformance, or activation outside assigned authority.
- Passing secrets, hidden reasoning, or unrestricted context through handoffs.

## Handoff minimum

- Source and destination role
- Objective, scope, baseline, and status
- Artifacts and evidence references
- Decisions and assumptions
- Risks, limitations, and unresolved items
- Required next action and acceptance criteria
