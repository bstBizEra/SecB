# Industry Research

## Workflow

1. Define industry boundary and adjacent sectors.
2. Map value chain and major participant roles.
3. Assess structure, concentration, economics, barriers, and substitutes.
4. Research regulation and policy using primary sources where possible.
5. Identify technology and business-model disruption signals.
6. Synthesize implications for the target decision.

## Required outputs

- industry definition
- structure/value chain
- major participants
- economics and barriers
- regulatory evidence
- disruption signals
- strategic implications
