# Competitive Intelligence

## Workflow

1. Define the competitive frame and substitute set.
2. Discover competitors from multiple independent channels.
3. Build company/product dossiers from primary sources where possible.
4. Compare features, pricing, positioning, customers, channels, and strategic signals.
5. Separate confirmed facts from inference.
6. Identify gaps, threats, differentiators, and monitoring signals.

## Required outputs

- competitor set and rationale
- competitor dossiers
- comparison matrix
- positioning/pricing evidence when available
- strategic signals
- gaps and threats
- confidence and evidence limitations
