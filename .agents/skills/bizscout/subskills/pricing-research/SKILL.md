# Pricing Research

## Workflow

1. Define product scope and comparable alternatives.
2. Capture current public pricing from primary sources where available.
3. Normalize billing period, currency, units, limits, discounts, and packaging.
4. Compare monetization models and price positioning.
5. Collect willingness-to-pay evidence only where defensible.
6. State missing or stale pricing explicitly.

## Required outputs

- pricing landscape
- normalized comparison
- packaging/monetization analysis
- evidence dates
- pricing implications
- confidence and limitations
