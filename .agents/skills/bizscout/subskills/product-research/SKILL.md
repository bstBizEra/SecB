# Product Research

## Scope

Use for product discovery, product landscape analysis, feature comparison, reviews, product gaps, pricing, positioning, and validation.

## Workflow

1. Define target customer and product problem.
2. Discover direct and indirect alternatives.
3. Compare product capabilities and positioning.
4. Mine reviews, communities, and other customer evidence where appropriate.
5. Analyze pricing and packaging with dated evidence.
6. Identify unmet needs and evidence-backed gaps.
7. Map assumptions and validation experiments.

## Required outputs

- product landscape
- customer problem evidence
- feature/alternative comparison
- pricing evidence when relevant
- product gaps/opportunities
- validation risks
- recommendation with confidence
