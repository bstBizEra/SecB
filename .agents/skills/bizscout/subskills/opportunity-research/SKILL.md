# Opportunity Research

## Workflow

1. Define the opportunity thesis.
2. Assemble evidence from relevant BizScout sub-skills.
3. Separate observed demand from inferred opportunity.
4. Assess market attractiveness, customer pain, competitive intensity, differentiation, economics, and execution constraints.
5. Identify key assumptions and validation experiments.
6. Rank opportunities using an explicit scoring model.
7. Produce a bounded recommendation, not an approval.

## Required outputs

- opportunity thesis
- supporting evidence
- attractiveness factors
- key risks and assumptions
- validation plan
- opportunity ranking/score with method
- recommendation and confidence
