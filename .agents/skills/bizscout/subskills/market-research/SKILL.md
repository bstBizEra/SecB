# Market Research

## Scope

Use for questions about market size, growth, segments, demand, industry attractiveness, addressable opportunity, and market entry.

## Workflow

1. Define the market boundary and exclusions.
2. Identify geography, customer segment, product/service category, and time period.
3. Collect primary and high-quality secondary evidence.
4. Build top-down sizing where data supports it.
5. Build bottom-up sizing where inputs support it.
6. Reconcile differences and expose assumptions.
7. Analyze growth, segmentation, demand, competition, barriers, and entry conditions.
8. Produce an attractiveness assessment and evidence-backed next step.

## Quantitative rules

Never present an estimate as a sourced fact. Show units, period, geography, currency, formula, and assumptions. When TAM/SAM/SOM is requested, triangulate top-down and bottom-up methods when feasible.

## Required outputs

- market definition
- market size or explicit sizing limitation
- growth/demand evidence
- segmentation
- attractiveness drivers and barriers
- risks and unknowns
- evidence-backed conclusion
