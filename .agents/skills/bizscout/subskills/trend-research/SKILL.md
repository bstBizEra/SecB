# Trend Research

## Workflow

1. Define the trend domain and time horizon.
2. Collect dated signals from independent sources.
3. Distinguish a one-off event from a repeated pattern.
4. Look for corroborating signals across channels.
5. Identify drivers, beneficiaries, risks, and counter-signals.
6. State confidence and what would falsify the trend thesis.

## Required outputs

- trend statement
- dated evidence/signals
- corroboration and counter-signals
- drivers
- likely implications
- confidence and monitoring indicators
