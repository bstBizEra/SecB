# Customer Research

## Workflow

1. Define customer population and segment boundaries.
2. Identify ICP characteristics and decision context.
3. Research jobs, pains, desired outcomes, and buying triggers.
4. Use direct customer evidence where available.
5. Mine reviews and communities carefully, distinguishing anecdotes from prevalence.
6. Synthesize themes and contradictions.
7. Translate findings into product, positioning, or validation implications.

## Required outputs

- target segment/ICP
- customer jobs and pain points
- evidence-backed needs
- buying/decision signals when available
- sentiment/themes
- uncertainty and sample limitations
- implications and next action
