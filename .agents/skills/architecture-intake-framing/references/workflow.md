# Detailed Workflow — Architecture Intake and Framing

## Preconditions

- Confirm: project or change intent.
- Confirm: available business context.
- Confirm: project contract or registration package.
- Confirm: context receipt when available.

## Detailed checks

- Identify the decision or outcome the architecture must enable.
- Separate verified facts, reported facts, assumptions, hypotheses, and unresolved questions.
- Map stakeholders, owners, users, operators, affected parties, and decision authorities.
- Define the system boundary, in-scope capabilities, exclusions, dependencies, and constraints.
- Classify risk and identify required specialist roles and downstream architecture skills.
- Define measurable success criteria and architecture deliverables.
- Produce an architecture brief and an unresolved-question register.

## Anti-patterns

- Starting implementation before the architecture scope and authority are established.
- Treating plausible inference as verified fact.
- Hiding uncertainty or adverse consequences.
- Using a framework mechanically when it does not answer the stakeholder question.
- Declaring approval, conformance, or activation outside assigned authority.
- Passing secrets, hidden reasoning, or unrestricted context through handoffs.

## Handoff minimum

- Source and destination role
- Objective, scope, baseline, and status
- Artifacts and evidence references
- Decisions and assumptions
- Risks, limitations, and unresolved items
- Required next action and acceptance criteria
