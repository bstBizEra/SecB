# Detailed Workflow — Stakeholder and Capability Mapping

## Preconditions

- Confirm: business objectives.
- Confirm: stakeholder list.
- Confirm: operating model.
- Confirm: project profile.

## Detailed checks

- Identify outcome owners, decision authorities, operators, users, regulators, and affected stakeholders.
- Translate objectives into business and platform capabilities.
- Assign accountable owners and supporting roles to capabilities.
- Map stakeholder concerns to architecture views, quality attributes, and evidence.
- Identify ownership gaps, overlapping authority, and separation-of-duties conflicts.
- Prioritize capabilities by value, risk, dependency, and implementation horizon.
- Produce a capability map and architecture concern matrix.

## Anti-patterns

- Starting implementation before the architecture scope and authority are established.
- Treating plausible inference as verified fact.
- Hiding uncertainty or adverse consequences.
- Using a framework mechanically when it does not answer the stakeholder question.
- Declaring approval, conformance, or activation outside assigned authority.
- Passing secrets, hidden reasoning, or unrestricted context through handoffs.

## Handoff minimum

- Source and destination role
- Objective, scope, baseline, and status
- Artifacts and evidence references
- Decisions and assumptions
- Risks, limitations, and unresolved items
- Required next action and acceptance criteria
